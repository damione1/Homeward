# Everyday controls

As a resident, I open a room from Home, scan compact groups and open the exact fan, light, thermostat or player when I need its controls. Back restores my originating room and position; closing a room returns to its parent. The visible control choices come from its admitted capabilities.

# Shopping

As a shopper, I open Shopping List from Home and check purchases without editing controls. As a planner, I open Lists, select a list, type an item or reactivate a previously completed item from Common Items in one tap. Switching lists cancels gestures captured for the previous list. An unknown write outcome is never retried automatically.

# Connection

As a resident, I expect an idle connected Home to stay available through normal watch rotations. Authority withdrawal clears private content. A synthetic command becomes confirmed only after the isolated driver publishes its causal state observation.
